How it works

Twenty cards at a time. No scrolling, no guilt.

Most of a photo library is the middle nobody ever scrolls back to. Keepworthy brings it to you a few cards at a time.

Deal

Get 20 or 50 random photos and videos you haven’t judged yet, from anywhere in your library.

Swipe

Right to keep, left to bin. Swipe up to see when and where a photo was taken, and the shots around it.

Empty the bin

Rescue anything you change your mind about. Emptying asks iOS first, and deleted photos wait 30 days in Recently Deleted.

Space cleared

See the space come back.

Every photo you bin counts toward the next cheaper iCloud+ plan. Tell Keepworthy which plan you’re on and it shows how far you are from dropping a tier, and what that would save you in a year.

It never shows a dollar figure without your plan.

Duplicate finder

Near-duplicates, sorted. PRO

Keepworthy finds shots taken moments apart that look alike, marks the best one, and bins the rest in one tap. It all happens on your iPhone; nothing is uploaded.

Scanning is free, and so is binning from your first 3 sets.

Rewind

Fall for old photos again.

Rewind deals from your whole archive, years back. Love a photo and it’s added to your Favorites. Turn on On this day for memories from today’s date in earlier years.

The bin

Nothing goes until you say so.

Swiping left only puts a photo in Keepworthy’s bin. Empty it when you’re ready, and iOS asks you to confirm. Keepworthy shows the day that space really comes back, and can tell you when it does.

Pricing
