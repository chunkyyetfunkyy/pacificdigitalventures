A strange bird

— Honolulu Star-Advertiser, September 2010

Korean tapas and flavored soju in a small, windowless bar on the back streets of Kakaʻako, a block off Ward. Same owners since 2010.

Brothers-in-law Jin Hong and Henry Yoon took the place over in 2010, changed the décor, and kept the name.

Duck butt in Korean is 오리궁뎅이, oh ri goong deng ee, a cute word for the shape of a behind. They kept the name because it was catchy and easy to remember. Some suppliers giggled when he said it.

Up front: booths, a bar, and K-pop videos on the screens. In the back: the private rooms.

The rooms

Private karaoke rooms in the back. Guides say the song lists run deep in English, Japanese and Korean, and the rooms go until close. K-pop videos play on the screens out front.

Rooms have a couch, the machine, and a phone to call for the check.

Birthdays, bachelorettes, after-work groups. Fridays and Saturdays, call ahead — reviewers keep saying it. If you’re not booking a room, there’s seating by the bar.
