A strange bird

— Honolulu Star-Advertiser, September 2010

Korean tapas and flavored soju in a small, windowless bar on the back streets of Kakaʻako, a block off Ward. Same owners since 2010.

Brothers-in-law Jin Hong and Henry Yoon took the place over in 2010, changed the décor, and kept the name.

The name is 오리궁뎅이, oh ri goong deng ee, a cute word for the shape of a behind. They kept it because it was catchy and easy to remember. Suppliers giggled. It worked.

Up front: booths, a bar, and K-pop videos on the screens. In the back: the private rooms.

노래방 noraebang · a private singing room

The rooms

Private karaoke rooms in the back, going until close. Songs in English, Japanese and Korean. K-pop videos on the screens out front.

Each room has a couch, the machine, and a phone on the wall. Pick it up for more soju or the check.

Birthdays, bachelorettes, after-work groups. Fridays and Saturdays, call ahead — regulars and every guide say so. If you’d rather watch, there’s seating by the bar.
